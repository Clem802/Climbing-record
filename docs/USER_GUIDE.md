# Spider Climbing Tracker — User Guide

Track your indoor boulder sessions, earn points, and watch your progress grow.

---

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Open the app and click **Register**
2. Enter your name, email, and a password (8+ characters)
3. You're in — your account starts on a **trial** subscription

> **Note:** If your account becomes inactive, you can still view past sessions but can't log new ones. Contact your administrator to reactivate.

---

## Logging a Session

### 1. Start a New Session

From the Dashboard, tap **+ Log New Session**.

### 2. Fill in Session Details

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Date** | The date you climbed (defaults to today) |
| **Location** | The gym or wall you climbed at |
| **Notes** | Optional — conditions, goals, how you felt |

### 3. Record Your Boulders

The grid shows **35 boulders** (numbered 1–35). For each boulder you attempted, tap the number of attempts it took:

| Button | Meaning | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Flashed it — topped first try | 10 pts |
| **2** | Topped on second attempt | 7 pts |
| **3** | Topped on third attempt | 4 pts |
| **4+** | Topped on fourth attempt or more | 1 pt |
| **—** | Not attempted (default) | 0 pts |

**Tips:**
- Tap a selected button again to deselect it (mark as not attempted)
- The **summary bar** at the bottom updates live as you log boulders
- Only boulders you topped count toward your score — don't log attempts on problems you didn't finish

### 4. Save Your Session

Tap **Save Session** when done. You'll see a summary card showing:
- Total points
- Boulders completed (out of 35)
- Number of flashes
- Average attempts per completed boulder

Tap **Done** to return to the Dashboard.

---

## Scoring System

Your score is the sum of points across all topped boulders in a session.

| Attempts to top | Points |
|---|---|
| 1 (flash) | 10 |
| 2 | 7 |
| 3 | 4 |
| 4 or more | 1 |

**Maximum possible score: 350 points** (all 35 boulders flashed)

---

## Viewing & Editing Past Sessions

All your sessions are listed on the Dashboard, newest first. Tap any row to open it.

From a session detail view you can:
- Change the date, location, or notes
- Update boulder attempts
- Tap **Save Changes** to update

> **Read-only mode:** If your subscription is inactive, you can view sessions but not edit them.

---

## Progress Charts

Tap **Progress** in the top nav to see five charts across your climbing history:

| Chart | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Score Over Time** | Your total session points across sessions |
| **Completion Rate** | Percentage of the 35 boulders you topped per session |
| **Flash Rate** | Number of first-attempt sends per session |
| **Avg Attempts** | Average attempts per topped boulder (lower = better) |
| **Points Breakdown** | Stacked bar showing how your points split across scoring tiers |

### Filtering by Date Range

Use the buttons in the top-right to filter:
- **Last 30 days**
- **Last 90 days**
- **All time**

## Dashboard Overview

The Dashboard is your home base:

- **Top nav** — links to Progress and Admin (if applicable), plus Sign out
- **Log New Session button** — starts a new session (requires active/trial subscription)
- **Session history table** — all past sessions with date, location, points, completed count, and flashes
- Click any session row to open and edit it

---

## Subscription Status

Your account can have one of three subscription states:

| Status | Can log new sessions? | Can view/edit past sessions? |
|---|---|---|
| **Active** | Yes | Yes |
| **Trial** | Yes | Yes |
| **Inactive** | No | View only |

If your status is inactive, you'll see an upgrade prompt on the Dashboard. Contact your administrator to change your status.

---

## Admin Panel

> Admin accounts only.

Access the Admin Panel via the **Admin** link in the top nav.

### Stats Cards
Shows total users, active subscribers, and total sessions logged across all users.

### User Table

For each user you can see:
- Name and email
- Role (admin / user)
- Subscription status — **click to cycle** through active → inactive → trial → active
- Total sessions logged
- Date of last session

### Actions

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| **Change subscription status** | Click the status badge — cycles through active / inactive / trial |
| **View a user's sessions** | Click **Sessions** — opens a panel on the right |
| **Delete a user** | Click **Delete** — you'll be asked to confirm. This cannot be undone. |

> You cannot delete your own admin account.

## Tips & Best Practices

- **Log as you climb** — it's easier to track attempts in real time than reconstruct from memory
- **Use notes** — record things like "tried the overhang project" or "warm-up only" for context
- **Check Progress regularly** — the avg attempts chart is one of the best indicators of improvement
- **Flashes matter** — even one flash per session adds up fast over time

## FAQ

**Can I log a boulder I attempted but didn't top?**
No — the scoring system only tracks topped boulders. The `—` button means "not attempted or not topped."

**What if I topped a boulder but can't remember how many attempts?**
Use **4+** as a conservative fallback — it still logs the send and awards 1 point.

**Can I go back and fix a session?**
Yes — tap any session on the Dashboard to open and edit it (requires active or trial subscription).

**Why can't I log a new session?**
Your subscription is inactive. Contact your administrator to reactivate your account.

**The Progress page shows no data for a date range.**
You have no sessions within that range. Try switching to **All time**.
